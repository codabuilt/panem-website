## Stop Wasting Food. Stop Wasting Money.

The average American family of four wastes nearly $3,000 a year on food that never gets eaten[^1] — that's over 10% of the typical food budget, gone. Panem is built to change that.

Panem helps you track what you have, use it before it expires, and shop smarter — so less food ends up in the trash and more stays in your pocket.

[Download Panem App](#)

## Built to Reduce Friction, Not Add It

Eliminating food waste only works if it's easy to stick with. Every Panem feature is designed to lower the effort it takes to stay on top of what you have:

- **Track everything in one place** — pantry, fridge, freezer, and more — so nothing gets forgotten and quietly expires
- **Expiration alerts** remind you before food goes bad, not after
- **Barcode scanning** makes adding items fast, so tracking doesn't feel like a chore
- **Shopping lists** help you plan ahead and replace only what you need — no more duplicate buys or overstocking
- **Dark and Light theme modes** for a clean experience, your way

## What's Next

We're just getting started on reducing friction even further:

- **Recipe suggestions** based on what's already in your kitchen, so you use up what's about to expire — coming soon
- **AI-powered photo import** — snap a picture of your cart or pantry shelf and let Panem add the items for you
- **Gamification** to help build and sustain better habits over time
